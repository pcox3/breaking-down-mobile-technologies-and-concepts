
## An Understanding Of System Design as a Mobile Developer.

Architecture is the structural design philosophy that shapes the outcome of a system. It governs the rules, trade-offs and direction. It could also be defined as the conceptual design of a system or structure, shaped and governed by principles that define how parts interact, scale and serve a purpose. Its about the vision and strcuture, built with purpose and guided by principle.</br></br>

Architecture consider:

- Form: What it looks like
- Function: What it does
- Flow: How it works over time. </br></br>

Arhitecture deems it necessary for a system or structure to have multiple parts and these parts need to work in harmony to serve the architecture's purpose. This is one of the famous architecture philosophy called **Saparation Of Concerns**. This introduces a misconception that because architecture introduces multiple parts, these parts automatically live in harmoney. NO they DON'T. The harmony of these parts is archieved through a system design.</br></br>


As the system keeps adding more parts also known as components, complexity is introduced; but it is important to note that, complexity doesn't always mean deificult. I like to use the statement that, **There's Simplicity in Complexity**; and this is possible because of saparation of concerns that was mentioned above. This philosophy also empasises that every part should have a focus or should do just one thing; making it easy to address future concerns.</br></br>

In conclusion, it is important to note that architecutre defines the scope of a system. Anything not covered in that scope, cannot be added. Scope  do change as need and requirements evolve, and as such scalability becomes the the primary characteristic of any architecture.</br></br>

So when thinking architecture, always remember these keywords: **Philosophy**, and **Trade-offs**
